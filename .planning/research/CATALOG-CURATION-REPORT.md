# Wholesale Apparel Catalog Curation Report

> Research compiled March 2026 for a custom decoration business curating ~1,200 styles
> down to ~5 products per category per brand.

---

## 1. Most Popular Garment Categories in Custom Apparel

The decorated apparel market is valued at approximately $38.7B (2025), growing at 13.1% CAGR.
The blank apparel market alone was $15.23B in 2024.

### Category Sales Breakdown

| Category | Est. Revenue Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **T-Shirts & Tanks** | ~48% | Dominant category. 48.39% of blank apparel revenue in 2024. Universal appeal. |
| **Hoodies & Sweatshirts** | ~20-25% | Second largest. ~25% of print-on-demand Q4 sales. Growing at 6.0% CAGR. |
| **Polos** | ~8-10% | Staple for corporate, uniforms, golf/country club. Steady demand. |
| **Headwear (Hats/Caps)** | ~6-8% | Compounding at 6.5% CAGR. High-margin accessory. |
| **Outerwear (Jackets/Vests)** | ~5-7% | Seasonal but high AOV. Corporate and team demand. |
| **Bottoms (Joggers/Shorts/Sweatpants)** | ~4-6% | Fast-growing via athleisure trend. |
| **Long Sleeve Tees** | ~3-5% | Seasonal bridge between tees and fleece. |
| **Accessories (Bags/Towels)** | ~2-4% | Lower volume but high margin and repeat orders. |

**Key insight:** T-shirts alone account for nearly half of all blank apparel revenue. However,
hoodies and fleece are the highest-margin category (45-60% profit margins on premium items vs
20-30% for standard tees) and should not be underrepresented in the catalog.

## 2. Key Differentiators That Matter

When customers choose between seemingly identical blank garments, these are the factors
that actually drive decisions, ranked by importance:

### 2.1 Fabric Composition

| Composition | Customer Perception | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **100% Combed Ring-Spun Cotton** | Soft, premium, natural feel | DTG, screen print, everyday retail |
| **100% Airlume Combed Ring-Spun** | Ultra-soft, retail-quality (Bella+Canvas proprietary) | Premium retail, fashion brands |
| **CVC (60% Cotton / 40% Polyester)** | Soft with durability, reduced shrinkage | Uniforms, repeat-wash applications |
| **Tri-Blend (50/25/25 Cotton/Poly/Rayon)** | Luxury hand-feel, heathered look, drape | Retail merch, fashion-forward brands |
| **50/50 Cotton/Polyester** | Durable, affordable, wrinkle-resistant | Budget promos, events, giveaways |
| **100% Polyester** | Moisture-wicking, sublimation-ready | Athletic, performance, sublimation |
| **100% Polyester (Heather)** | Performance with cotton-like appearance | Sublimation with retail look |

**Rule of thumb:** Ring-spun cotton always beats open-end cotton in perceived quality. Open-end
cotton (like classic Gildan 5000) feels rougher and is positioned purely as budget/promotional.

### 2.2 Fabric Weight

| Weight Class | oz/yd² | GSM | Use Case | Popularity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lightweight** | 3.5-4.3 oz | 120-145 GSM | Summer events, fashion, layering | Growing (fashion trend) |
| **Midweight** | 4.3-5.3 oz | 145-180 GSM | Year-round workhorse, uniforms, most printing | **Most popular** |
| **Heavyweight** | 5.5-6.1+ oz | 185-200+ GSM | Premium/streetwear, structured look, embroidery | Fast-growing trend in 2025-2026 |

**The sweet spot for most custom work is 4.2-5.3 oz.** Heavyweight (6+ oz) is trending strongly
for streetwear-inspired and premium branded merchandise.

### 2.3 Fit

| Fit | Description | Trend Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular/Classic** | Traditional straight cut. Tubular or side-seamed. | Stable |
| **Retail/Modern** | Slightly tapered, side-seamed. Resembles store-bought tees. | **Growing strongly** |
| **Slim/Fitted** | Close to body, fashionable. Common in Bella+Canvas/Next Level. | Stable |
| **Relaxed/Boxy/Oversized** | Dropped shoulder, wide body. Streetwear-inspired. | **Fastest growing 2025-2026** |
| **Cropped** | Women's fashion trend. Shorter hem. | Niche but growing |

**The industry has shifted decisively toward side-seamed, retail-fit garments.** Tubular
construction is now perceived as "cheap" by most customers outside pure promotional use.

### 2.4 Neckline

- **Crew neck**: 70%+ of all t-shirt sales. The default.
- **V-neck**: 10-15%. Declining slightly but still essential to offer.
- **Scoop neck**: Niche, primarily women's fashion.
- **Henley**: Very niche. Skip unless specifically requested.

### 2.5 Construction Features That Signal Quality

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| **Side-seamed** | Modern fit, retail feel. Industry standard for premium blanks. |
| **Tear-away label** | Enables private labeling / rebranding. Expected on premium blanks. |
| **Shoulder taping** | Prevents stretching, maintains shape after washing. |
| **Double-needle stitching** | Durability signal at hem and sleeves. |
| **Set-in sleeve vs. raglan** | Set-in is standard; raglan offers distinct athletic look. |

## 5. Selection Criteria for "Best 5 Per Category Per Brand"

### 5.1 Selection Dimensions

Each product should be evaluated across these dimensions:

#### A. Color Availability
- **Excellent:** 30+ colors
- **Good:** 20-29 colors
- **Adequate:** 10-19 colors
- **Poor:** Under 10 colors

#### B. Size Range
- **Excellent:** XS-5XL or XS-6XL (inclusive sizing)
- **Good:** S-3XL
- **Adequate:** S-2XL
- **Poor:** S-XL only

#### C. Price Point Positioning
Ensure the 5 selections cover the spectrum:
- 1-2 Budget tier ($2-4 wholesale)
- 2-3 Mid tier ($4-7 wholesale)
- 1-2 Premium tier ($7-12+ wholesale)

#### D. Fabric Differentiation
The 5 selections should NOT overlap on composition. Ideal spread:
1. 100% Cotton (ring-spun) — the workhorse
2. CVC or 50/50 blend — the durable option
3. Tri-blend — the premium/fashion option
4. 100% Polyester or performance blend — the athletic/sublimation option
5. Heavyweight or specialty — the trend/streetwear option

#### E. Fit Differentiation
Avoid selecting 5 products with identical fits:
1. Classic/regular fit
2. Retail/modern fit
3. Relaxed/oversized (if available)
4. Slim/fitted (if the brand offers it)
5. Special cut (cropped, boxy, athletic)

#### F. Decoration Compatibility
Each product should be tagged with compatible methods:
- **Screen Print:** Works on virtually everything. Prefer 4.5+ oz cotton/blends.
- **DTF/Heat Transfer:** Works on all fabrics. Excellent versatility.
- **DTG (Direct to Garment):** Best on 100% combed ring-spun cotton. Light-colored preferred.
- **Embroidery:** Needs 4.5+ oz structured fabric. Best on polos, fleece, outerwear.
- **Sublimation:** Requires 65%+ polyester content. White or light base only.

#### G. Unique Features
Products that offer differentiating features score higher:
- Tear-away labels (relabeling capability)
- Side-seamed construction
- Shoulder taping
- Pre-shrunk treatment
- WRAP or OEKO-TEX certification
- Retail-ready packaging option

#### H. Stock Reliability
Based on historical availability from major distributors (S&S, SanMar, alphabroder):
- Consistent year-round availability
- Fast replenishment cycles
- Core styles vs seasonal/limited runs

## 6. Recommended Category Taxonomy

### Top-Level Structure

```
Apparel
├── T-Shirts
│   ├── Short Sleeve
│   ├── Long Sleeve
│   ├── Tank Tops & Sleeveless
│   ├── V-Neck
│   ├── Performance / Moisture-Wicking
│   └── Crop Tops
│
├── Fleece & Sweatshirts
│   ├── Pullover Hoodies
│   ├── Zip Hoodies (Full & Quarter)
│   ├── Crewneck Sweatshirts
│   └── Quarter-Zip Pullovers
│
├── Polos & Dress Shirts
│   ├── Short Sleeve Polos
│   ├── Long Sleeve Polos
│   └── Dress / Button-Down Shirts
│
├── Outerwear
│   ├── Jackets (Softshell, Windbreaker)
│   ├── Vests
│   ├── Rain & Shell Jackets
│   └── Fleece Jackets
│
├── Bottoms
│   ├── Sweatpants & Joggers
│   ├── Shorts
│   └── Athletic / Performance Pants
│
├── Youth
│   ├── T-Shirts
│   ├── Fleece
│   └── Polos
│
└── Accessories
    ├── Hats & Caps (Structured, Unstructured, Beanies)
    ├── Bags & Totes
    ├── Towels
    ├── Aprons
    └── Face Coverings / Neck Gaiters
```

### Taxonomy Principles

1. **Brand is an attribute, not a category.** Never organize by brand at the top level.
2. **Material/fabric is an attribute, not a category.** Use filterable metadata.
3. **Gender is an attribute.** Use Unisex as the default. Only separate Men's/Women's when
   the cut is genuinely different (e.g., women's fitted vs unisex).
4. **Season is a tag.** Do not create "Summer T-Shirts" categories.
5. **Depth limit: 3 levels.** Category > Subcategory > (optional) Sub-subcategory.
6. **Decoration method compatibility** should be a filterable attribute on every product.

## 7. Scoring Model for Product Selection

### Formula

```
TOTAL_SCORE = (colorCount_norm * 3.0)
            + (sizeRange_norm * 2.5)
            + (fabricDifferentiation * 2.0)
            + (decorationVersatility * 2.0)
            + (stockReliability_norm * 2.0)
            + (priceCompetitiveness_norm * 1.5)
            + (uniqueFeatures * 1.5)
            + (fitModernity * 1.5)
            + (trendAlignment * 1.0)

Max possible: 100 points (each normalized component is 0-~6 before weighting)
```

### Component Definitions

#### colorCount_norm (weight: 3.0)
More colors = more customer choices = more orders.

| Colors Available | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| 40+ | 6 |
| 30-39 | 5 |
| 20-29 | 4 |
| 15-19 | 3 |
| 10-14 | 2 |
| 5-9 | 1 |
| Under 5 | 0 |

#### sizeRange_norm (weight: 2.5)
Inclusive sizing wins more orders. Corporate and team orders often need 3XL+.

| Size Range | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| XS-5XL or wider | 6 |
| XS-4XL | 5 |
| S-3XL | 4 |
| S-2XL | 3 |
| S-XL | 2 |
| Limited | 1 |

#### fabricDifferentiation (weight: 2.0)
Does this product fill a unique fabric niche within the brand's 5 selections?

| Criteria | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Unique composition not covered by other selections | 6 |
| Partially overlaps but distinct weight or feel | 3 |
| Redundant with another selection | 0 |

#### decorationVersatility (weight: 2.0)
How many decoration methods work well on this product?

| Compatible Methods | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| 4+ methods (screen, DTF, DTG, embroidery, sublimation) | 6 |
| 3 methods | 4 |
| 2 methods | 2 |
| 1 method only | 1 |

#### stockReliability_norm (weight: 2.0)
Based on distributor availability data.

| Availability | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Core style, always in stock at major distributors | 6 |
| Usually available, occasional stockouts in some sizes/colors | 4 |
| Frequently out of stock or seasonal | 2 |
| Unreliable / new product with no track record | 1 |

#### priceCompetitiveness_norm (weight: 1.5)
Relative to the category average.

| Price Position | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Budget (below category average, fills price gap) | 5 |
| Mid-range (at category average) | 4 |
| Premium (above average, justified by features) | 4 |
| Overpriced (above average, no clear differentiator) | 1 |

#### uniqueFeatures (weight: 1.5)
Cumulative score for premium construction features.

| Feature | Points |
|---|---|
| Side-seamed construction | +1 |
| Tear-away label | +1 |
| Shoulder taping | +1 |
| Pre-shrunk | +0.5 |
| OEKO-TEX / sustainability cert | +1 |
| Retail-ready packaging | +0.5 |
| Double-needle stitching | +0.5 |

(Cap at 6)

#### fitModernity (weight: 1.5)
How well does the fit align with current market demand?

| Fit Type | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Retail/modern side-seamed | 6 |
| Relaxed/oversized (trending) | 5 |
| Classic with side seams | 4 |
| Tubular classic | 2 |

#### trendAlignment (weight: 1.0)
Does this product align with 2025-2026 market trends?

| Alignment | Raw Score |
|---|---|
| Directly addresses a strong trend (heavyweight, oversized, sustainable) | 6 |
| Partially aligns with trends | 3 |
| No particular trend relevance | 1 |

### Selection Algorithm

For each **category + brand** combination:

1. **Score all products** in that category from that brand using the formula above.
2. **Rank by total score** descending.
3. **Apply diversity filter:** Before taking the top 5, ensure the selections cover:
   - At least 2 different fabric compositions
   - At least 2 different price tiers
   - At least 2 different fit profiles
   - If any top-5 candidate is redundant (same fabric + same fit + similar price as another
     already selected), skip it and take the next-ranked product instead.
4. **Output the final 5** with scores and rationale.

### Example: Selecting 5 Bella+Canvas T-Shirts

| # | Style | Composition | Weight | Fit | Score | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BC 3001 | 100% Airlume Cotton | 4.2 oz | Retail | 92 | Flagship cotton tee |
| 2 | BC 3413 | Tri-Blend | 3.8 oz | Retail | 87 | Premium fashion tee |
| 3 | BC 3001CVC | CVC 52/48 | 4.2 oz | Retail | 82 | Durable blend option |
| 4 | BC 3010 | 100% Airlume Cotton | 4.2 oz | Heavyweight/Boxy | 78 | Trend: oversized/streetwear |
| 5 | BC 6400 | 100% Airlume Cotton | 4.2 oz | Women's Relaxed | 75 | Women's-specific cut |

### Top 2025-2026 Trends

#### 1. Heavyweight Renaissance
Heavyweight tees (6+ oz) and premium fleece are surging. Streetwear influence has made
thick, structured garments desirable. Brands like Los Angeles Apparel and Bella+Canvas
FWD Fashion line are capitalizing on this.

**Action:** Include at least one heavyweight option per brand in your t-shirt and fleece
selections.

#### 2. Oversized / Relaxed Fits
Boxy, dropped-shoulder silhouettes dominate youth and fashion markets. Not just men's —
women's oversized is equally strong.

**Action:** Prioritize products with relaxed/oversized fit options alongside classic fits.

#### 3. Sustainability as Table Stakes
Organic cotton, recycled polyester, and OEKO-TEX certification are no longer premium
differentiators — they are expected by a growing segment. Consumers, especially Gen Z
(now 18-28), actively seek brands with sustainability credentials.

**Action:** Ensure at least one sustainable option per brand (organic cotton, recycled poly,
or certified production).

#### 4. Athleisure Crossover
The line between athletic and casual continues to blur. Performance fabrics (moisture-wicking,
4-way stretch) are now expected in casual categories. Joggers, performance polos, and
soft-shell jackets are crossing over from gym to office.

**Action:** Include performance/moisture-wicking options in polos, bottoms, and outerwear.

#### 5. DTF as the Universal Decoration Method
DTF (Direct-to-Film) has emerged as the most versatile decoration method, working on
virtually all fabrics and colors. This reduces the importance of fabric-specific decoration
constraints and shifts focus to fabric feel and fit.

**Action:** While all-fabric compatibility is great, still tag products for optimal methods
per fabric. 100% cotton still gives superior DTG results; polyester is still required for
sublimation.

#### 6. Inclusive Sizing Demand
Extended sizing (up to 5XL-6XL) is increasingly expected, especially in corporate and
team orders. Brands offering XS-5XL have a significant competitive advantage.

**Action:** Heavily weight size range in your scoring model. Deprioritize products that
cap at XL or 2XL.

#### 7. Minimalist Design Dominance
Data shows that products with 1-2 simple design elements outperform complex designs,
representing 56% of all eligible sales. This means the blank quality, fit, and color
matters MORE than ever — the garment itself is the product, not just a canvas.

**Action:** Invest in premium blanks. Customers notice and care about fabric quality
when designs are minimal.

## Summary: Quick-Reference Selection Checklist

When evaluating whether a product makes the top 5 for its category + brand:

- [ ] Does it fill a unique fabric/weight niche not covered by other selections?
- [ ] Does it have 20+ color options including core colors (black, white, gray, navy)?
- [ ] Does it offer sizing to at least 3XL?
- [ ] Is it side-seamed with tear-away labels?
- [ ] Is it reliably in stock at major distributors?
- [ ] Does it work with 3+ decoration methods?
- [ ] Does it fill a price tier (budget/mid/premium) not already covered?
- [ ] Does it align with at least one 2025-2026 trend?

If a product checks 6+ of these boxes, it belongs in the top 5. If it checks fewer than 4,
it is a candidate for removal from the catalog entirely.
