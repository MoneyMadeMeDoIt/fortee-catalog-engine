### Human Verification Required

#### 1. Visual garment-to-canvas ratio on real supplier images

**Test:** Run `pushProduct` against a real CSW or S&S Canada styleID with front/back images and inspect the resulting Shopify product images.
**Expected:** Garment occupies ~73% of canvas height for tops (79% for hoodies), centered horizontally, on a white background — matching the proportions of reference images S05280 and L00550.
**Why human:** Pixel-level ratio correctness cannot be verified without live supplier image URLs. Sharp trim behavior on real JPEG images (with JPEG compression artifacts near white edges) may differ from synthetic PNG test images.

#### 2. Print area coordinate accuracy on real products

**Test:** After pushing a product, check the `print_area_position` variant metafield JSON and compare the front print area bounding box against the actual garment in the image.
**Expected:** The front print area rectangle visually overlaps the garment's chest/torso region correctly, not shifted or scaled incorrectly.
**Why human:** `GARMENT_RELATIVE_PRINT_FRACTIONS` values (e.g., `xFrac: 0.15, wFrac: 0.70` for tops) were designed from reference images. Whether they correctly position the print area on different garment styles (boxy vs. fitted, long vs. short) requires visual inspection of real products.

#### 3. Fallback behavior on dark or non-white-background images

**Test:** Push a product with a garment image that has a non-white or dark background (if any CSW products have these).
**Expected:** The fallback (`fit:contain`) fires and produces a usable 2000x2000 image with a warning in logs; the product push does not crash.
**Why human:** The 70% safety threshold may need tuning depending on real image characteristics.
